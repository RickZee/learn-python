# Beginner path

For you if you have never coded, or only a little. Plan on about 4–6 hours a week.

## Checklist

1. [Install](../get-started/install.md) (or open Codespaces)
2. [First notebook](../get-started/first-notebook.md)
3. Work through the [course](../../course/README.md) in order, lesson then practice
4. When a lesson links to How Python works, open that [internals](../../internals/README.md) page after the course lesson
5. Skip challenges until you finish Part 1 through classes
6. Then pick one [project](../../use-cases/README.md)

## What to skip at first

- Challenge problems and pattern lessons until the course feels familiar
- Deep concurrency and tooling until you have written a few Part 2 exercises

Use an AI tool as your tutor: see [Learning with an AI tutor](../guides/learning-with-ai.md).
